# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, equal weight:

- Recruiters and hiring managers scanning in under a minute: who is this, what has he built, how do I reach him.
- Engineers and tech leads who want depth: how things work, what trade-offs were made, whether results were measured.

The page must be scannable first, with depth available on demand.

## Product Purpose

Get Kok Young interviews for senior engineering, AI tooling and platform roles. It does this by showing shipped work, with the pokérag and Snuggle demos up front, instead of listing skills. Success means a visitor can name one thing he built and how it works after a single visit.

## Brand Personality

Calm, refined, crafted, with one signature interaction. Quiet confidence; nothing shouts. Light, precise and minimal, with the project visuals carrying the colour. The signature is the project wheel: scroll to turn through the work, click to get the details instantly, in place.

## Anti-references

- Anything that reads as AI-generated or templated: SaaS landing-page grammar, hero-metric blocks, identical card grids, small caps labels above every section, generic sans plus one blue accent (the October 2026 Geist version was rejected for exactly this).
- Dark "product launch" clones (the earlier Raycast-style version).
- Gimmicks that perform skill instead of showing it: 3D blobs, fake terminals, typing animations.
- Copying the framing device of yijie-portfolio (an illustrated desk scene zooming into a monitor).
- Themed metaphors. A darkroom concept (contact sheets, safelight, developing prints) was rejected as too whimsical. Uniqueness comes from interaction craft, not a costume.

## References

- gabrielbeaugonin.com (primary). Take: one calm screen, name and role on the left, a vertical 3D wheel of project cards you scroll through, dot indicator, pale off-white with colour only in the work. Change: clicking a card must reveal the details immediately in place, not navigate to a new page.

- Personal dev sites: brittanychiang.com, leerob.com, paco.me. Take: personal and focused, a clear point of view, small details rewarded on a second look.
- yijie-portfolio.vercel.app. Take: the creativity, not the look. A personal world as the framing device and one scroll-driven moment that makes the site memorable, while the content itself stays simple.

## Design Principles

1. Show, don't claim. Real demos and real engineering decisions over adjectives.
2. Craft is the proof. Small details (type, spacing, interaction) carry the polish.
3. A person, not a product page.
4. One signature interaction, everything else calm. Motion is part of the story, not decoration.
5. Information on demand, instantly. Browsing is effortless; one click opens the full details with no page load.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full reduced-motion support (the signature effect must degrade to a calm crossfade or a static, fully visible state). Keyboard-usable video controls, labelled media, visible focus states. Content must never depend on an animation firing to become visible.
